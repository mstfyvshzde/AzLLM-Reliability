# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to communicate professionally, critique ideas rather
than people, and help maintain a research environment that is rigorous,
welcoming, and evidence-driven.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- giving specific technical feedback,
- acknowledging uncertainty,
- documenting methodological disagreements,
- correcting errors transparently,
- and respecting reproducibility and research-integrity requirements.

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, and attempts to conceal material experimental
changes are not acceptable.

## Research Integrity

Contributors should not:

- use held-out test data for model or checkpoint selection,
- silently alter frozen artifacts,
- misrepresent AI-assisted review as independent human review,
- remove inconvenient results without documentation,
- or report metrics without preserving enough provenance to reproduce them.

Methodological disagreements should be documented clearly and resolved through
evidence whenever possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate
these standards and may restrict participation when necessary.

For serious concerns, use private GitHub reporting channels when available.
